3 · Scale calibration

Rule 7(3) fixes declaration heights in millimetres, so the scan needs to know how many pixels make a millimetre. A.T.L.A.S. reads the pack's own EAN-13 barcode as an in-frame ruler — a GS1 symbol is 95 modules wide, 31.35 mm at nominal magnification. Nothing needs to be typed in when a barcode is visible.

Rule 7(3) sets a higher minimum height for embossed print.

What gets checked

All eight mandatory declarations under Rule 6, the "inclusive of all taxes" qualifier under Rule 18(2), the Rule 7(3) minimum heights for MRP and net quantity, Rule 26 and wholesale exemptions, an impossible manufacture date, and a cross-check between the barcode's GS1 country prefix and the declared country of origin.

If the capture is too poor to rely on, the scan returns INCONCLUSIVE and asks for a retake rather than guessing.
